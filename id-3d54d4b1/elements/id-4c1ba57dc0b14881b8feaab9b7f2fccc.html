Consent Management Service ()
Definition: Consent Management Service ABB is a technology service that captures, stores, manages, and enforces data subject consent declarations, ensuring that personal data processing operations are carried out in accordance with the expressed and recorded consent of the individual. Source: Industry practice (identity and access management, data protection compliance) Last modification: 2026-02-12 Identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/ConsentManagementTechnologyService LOST view: Technical view - infrastructure EIRA concept: eira:ArchitectureBuildingBlock ABB name: eira:ConsentManagementTechnologyService Synonym: Consent Registry Service , Data Subject Consent Service Interoperability Dimension: Structural IoP Interoperability Saliency: Supports interoperable operations by enabling consistent capture and enforcement of consent declarations across organisations and jurisdictions, independent of the underlying data processing system or sector. Example: Recording a citizen's explicit consent to share health data with a third-party provider, and enforcing withdrawal of that consent upon request across all connected systems. Additional identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/ConsentManagementTechnologyService Additional information: The scope is consent lifecycle management (capture, storage, enforcement, and withdrawal); identity verification and access control decisions are out of scope.
