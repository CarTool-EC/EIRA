AI Model Training Service ()
Definition: AI Model Training ABB is a technology service that trains machine learning or artificial intelligence models by processing labelled or unlabelled datasets to generate statistical, neural, or rule-based models for prediction, classification, or decision support. Last modification: 2026-01-26 Identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/AIModelTrainingTechnologyService LOST view: Technical view – infrastructure EIRA concept: eira:ArchitectureBuildingBlock ABB name: eira:AIModelTrainingTechnologyService Additional identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/AIModelTrainingTechnologyService Synonym: Machine Learning Training , Model Training , AI Training , Learning Service , Model Builder , Training Pipeline Example: Azure Machine Learning training, AWS SageMaker training jobs, AutoML platforms , national AI sandboxes Interoperability Saliency: AI Model Training enables interoperable digital public services to build, update, and validate machine learning models in a reusable, auditable, and portable manner across Member States. Additional information: AI Model Training provides a generic capability to create models that can be reused by multiple digital public services.
