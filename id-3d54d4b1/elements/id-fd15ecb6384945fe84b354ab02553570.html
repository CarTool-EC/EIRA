Recovery ()
Definition: A Recovery ABB is a technology service that enables the restoration of infrastructure, platforms, and services to an operational state after failures, cyber incidents, or disasters. It supports backup restoration, failover, and continuity mechanisms to ensure that digital public services can be resumed within defined recovery time and recovery point objectives. Last modification: 2026-01-26 Identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/RecoveryTechnologyService LOST view: Technical view Infrastructure EIRA concept: eira:ArchitectureBuildingBlock ABB name: eira:RecoveryTechnologyService Additional identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/RecoveryTechnologyService Synonym: Disaster Recovery , Service Recovery , System Recovery , Infrastructure Recovery , Business Continuity Support Example: Backup and restore platforms ,site failover systems , snapshot-based recovery , Azure Site Recovery ,AWS Elastic Disaster Recovery , on-premise DR systems Interoperability Saliency: The Recovery ABB is salient for technical interoperability because it enables continuity of digital public services across different infrastructures and jurisdictions. By abstracting recovery capabilities from specific hosting environments, it allows Member States to implement disaster recovery and failover strategies in cloud, on-premise, or hybrid facilities while preserving service availability and cross-border service reliability. Additional information: Recovery focuses on restoring technical service availability after incidents. It complements but does not replace business-level continuity planning or application-level fault-tolerance, which are handled in higher architecture layers.
