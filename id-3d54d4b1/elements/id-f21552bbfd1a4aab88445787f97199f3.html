Content Delivery Service ()
Definition: Content Delivery Service ABB is a Technology service that enables the efficient, reliable, and scalable delivery of digital content to consumers by managing content distribution, access and transport across networked infrastructures. It ensures that content is made available to end users and systems with appropriate performance, availability, and security characteristics, independent of how or where the content is stored. Source: Industry practice (content delivery networks and distributed content services) Last modification: 2026-02-02 Identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/ContentDeliveryServiceTechnologyService LOST view: Technical view - infrastructure EIRA concept: eira:ArchitectureBuildingBlock ABB name: eira:ContentDeliveryServiceTechnologyService Synonym: Content Distribution Service , Content Serving Service Example: Delivering public documents, images, videos, and web resources to citizens , providing secure download of large files such as legal documents or datasets. Interoperability Saliency: This ABB is salient for technical interoperability because it ensures that digital public services can reliably and efficiently distribute content to users and systems regardless of geographical location, network conditions, or hosting environments. Additional information: Content Delivery Service focuses on how content is distributed and accessed.
