AI Governance Service ()
Definition: AI Governance ABB is a technology service that establishes, enforces, and monitors legal, ethical, risk, and policy controls over artificial intelligence systems, ensuring that AI models and their use comply with regulatory, ethical, and organisational governance frameworks Last modification: 2026-01-26 Identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/AIGovernanceTechnologyService LOST view: Technical view – infrastructure EIRA concept: eira:ArchitectureBuildingBlock ABB name: eira:AIGovernanceTechnologyService Additional identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/AIGovernanceTechnologyService Synonym: AI Policy Management Service, AI Compliance Service, Responsible AI Governance Example: Azure Responsible AI , AWS AI Governance Toolkit , IBM AI Governance Interoperability Saliency: AI Governance service ensures that AI models exchanged or reused across public administrations comply with EU legal, ethical, and policy requirements, enabling trusted cross-border interoperability. Additional information: This ABB controls how AI models are approved, restricted, audited, and withdrawn based on risk classification, legal obligations, and ethical constraints. It works together with the AI Model Lifecycle Management and AI Model Registry services. Interoperability Dimension: Structural IoP EIF Layer: TechnicalInfrastructure
