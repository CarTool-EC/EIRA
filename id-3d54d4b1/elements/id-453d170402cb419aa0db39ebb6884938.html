Reporting and Dashboarding ()
Definition: Reporting and Dashboarding ABB is a technology service that presents, visualizes, and distributes information derived from analytics and data to support monitoring, and decision-making by public administrations, businesses, and citizens. Last modification: 2026-01-26 Identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/ReportingAndDashboardingTechnologyService LOST view: Technical view – infrastructure EIRA concept: eira:ArchitectureBuildingBlock ABB name: eira:ReportingAndDashboardingTechnologyService Additional identifier: http://data.europa.eu/dr8/ReportingAndDashboardingTechnologyService Synonym: Business Intelligence , Reporting , Data Visualization , Dashboards , KPI Monitoring , Management Reporting Example: Power BI , Tableau ,Grafana , data portals , KPI dashboards , financial reporting portals , tax transparency dashboards Interoperability Saliency: Reporting and Dashboarding enables interoperable digital public services to expose standardized performance indicators, financial transparency, and operational status across Member States. Additional information: Reporting and Dashboarding consumes outputs from Descriptive, Diagnostic, Predictive, and Prescriptive Analytics but does not perform analytical computation itself.
