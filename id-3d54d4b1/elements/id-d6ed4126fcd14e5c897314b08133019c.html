Observability and Monitoring Enablers ()
Definition: The Observability and Monitoring Enablers ABB is a Grouping that refers to application services and components that provide capabilities to observe, monitor, measure, analyse, and diagnose the runtime behaviour, performance, health, and reliability of Digital Service Infrastructures (DSIs). It encompasses capabilities for collecting, correlating, processing, and exposing telemetry signals (such as metrics, logs, traces, and events) in order to support proactive monitoring, incident detection, troubleshooting, optimisation, and reliability management of digital public services. By standardising how runtime information (metrics, logs, traces, events) is collected and accessed, it enables cross-system diagnostics, coordinated incident management, and reliable operation of interconnected Digital Service Infrastructures.
